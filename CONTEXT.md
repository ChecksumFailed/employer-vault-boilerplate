# Vault domain glossary

## Inbox note

An unclassified or unprocessed capture that has not yet earned a durable note type.

## Project hub

The curated dashboard for a project. It records current state, desired outcome, next steps, risks, and immediately important decisions while discovering child records through project links.

## Meeting note

The record of a time-bound interaction, including what changed, decisions, actions, open questions, and essential context.

## Research note

An active investigation that preserves a question, current understanding, evidence, sources, findings, and remaining uncertainty.

## Work item

A project concern that needs independent ownership, status, investigation, discussion, or history. Its kind is issue, enhancement, risk, or question.

## Decision note

The durable record of a consequential choice, its context, alternatives, rationale, consequences, and reconsideration conditions.

## Knowledge note

A synthesized, reusable explanation that remains useful outside the investigation or event that produced it.

## General note

Useful project context that does not meet the definition of a meeting, research investigation, work item, decision, or knowledge note.
